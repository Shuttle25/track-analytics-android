# Privacy Policy

**Track Analytics**
*Last updated: March 24, 2026*

## Overview

Track Analytics is a GPX track comparison tool that works entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any data.** Specifically:

- No personal information is collected
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No crash reporting
- No advertising or tracking
- No network connections of any kind

## File Access

The app reads GPX files that you explicitly select or share with it. These files are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

## Data Storage

Track data is held in memory only during app use. No data is stored permanently by the app beyond Android's standard app backup mechanism.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from any user, including children.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the changes will be posted here with a revised date.
